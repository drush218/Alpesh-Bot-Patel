## 9. Bottom line
BAU assumes 2026 is the margin trough (~7% after Brazil shipping discounts and upfront card provisions) and that margin rebuilds to ~10.5% by 2028 while revenue compounds ~30%. The deciding variables are provisions vs fintech revenue, BRL translation, and whether competition lets shipping subsidies roll off. Asymmetry: growth is robust in both cases; EPS hinges almost entirely on margin normalisation, and LOW stacks FX and credit losses together.
